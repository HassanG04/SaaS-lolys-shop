# Forecast dataset export

Owner-requested addition, 2026-10-29. Complements `analytics-plan.md`; no pricing model or automatic price changes are introduced.

The database requires an AAL2 admin and at least **56 days since the first valid sale plus 30 valid orders**, matching the shop gate. Requests with `p_export=false` return readiness only; `true` rechecks permissions and readiness before returning data. Orders must contain items, be confirmed/shipped/delivered COD or paid InstaPay, and not cancelled/rejected/expired. Only completed UTC days count, preventing partial-day training. Browser counts never authorize export.

Rows cover each product/day from its catalog creation date within the latest 730 completed UTC days, starting no earlier than the first valid sale. Zero-sale days and unsold products are included. Above 50,000 rows the operation fails without downloading a truncated file; a future offline export can address larger stores. Columns include product/category context, valid order count, units, revenue, quantity-weighted paid unit price, current list price/discount/stock, wishlist count at export, daily stock observations, calendar flags and export timestamp. No customer identifiers, contact details, addresses, order IDs or payment proofs are exported. Existing formula-safe CSV formatting is reused.

Current catalog fields and wishlist counts are **snapshots at export**, not historical features. Do not feed them into historical backtests as if known at the sale date. Paid unit prices are immutable order-item snapshots. Historical discounts cannot be recovered reliably by comparing an old sale with today's list price. No-sales days have a blank paid unit price; missing stock/calendar observations stay blank (unknown). A zero-sale day does not prove zero demand or availability. Known inventory movement days have stockout flags; fill gaps only with justified inventory history. Data cleaning, poisoning exclusions, outlier treatment and backtesting in the existing analytics pipeline remain required before modeling this raw aggregate export.

Category forecasts still require 84 days/40 units; product forecasts require 12 weeks with sales in 6 weeks. There is no universal optimal sample size for price recommendations: variation in actual prices/promotions, inventory and time-held-out evaluation matter. The download gate permits evaluation, not a claim of forecast accuracy or causal discount uplift. Production forecast activation remains human task T-9.13.
